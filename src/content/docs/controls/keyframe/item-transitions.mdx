---
title: Item Transitions
description: Switch off CollectionView's native item animations, play staggered appear and add animations on items, and animate an item out before removing it
---

`ItemTransitions` adds item animations to a `CollectionView` (or any `ItemsView`). It can switch the platform's insert, remove and move animations off, play a `Keyframes` on items as they first appear (staggered, for a list entrance) or as they are added, and animate an item out before it is removed.

```xml
<CollectionView ItemsSource="{Binding Items}"
                kf:ItemTransitions.Mode="None"
                kf:ItemTransitions.Appear="{StaticResource FadeUp}"
                kf:ItemTransitions.Add="{StaticResource PopIn}"
                kf:ItemTransitions.Remove="{StaticResource SlideOut}"
                kf:ItemTransitions.Stagger="0:0:0.04" />
```

```csharp
await ItemTransitions.RemoveAsync(List, item, () => Items.Remove(item));
```

| Attached property | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Mode` | `Platform` | `None` switches the native item animations off. |
| `Appear` | — | Played on each item the first time a view shows it. |
| `Add` | — | Played on items added to the source after load. Falls back to `Appear`. |
| `Remove` | — | Played by `RemoveAsync` before the item is removed. |
| `Stagger` | `0:0:0.04` | Delay between items that appear together. At most 12 steps are taken. |

## Notes

- **Appear plays once per item.** Scrolling back to an item doesn't replay it; a recycled view that shows a new item does. `Appear` and `Add` timelines get a backwards fill automatically, so a staggered item holds its opening pose instead of flashing in its final one.
- **Remove is a call, not a hook.** Once an item has left the source the platform tears its view down at once, so `RemoveAsync` plays the animation on the item's view first and then runs your removal. If the item isn't on screen, it is removed straight away.
- **Moves** use the platform's animation, or nothing under `Mode="None"`.
- **The off switch, by platform:** Android sets `SetItemAnimator(null)`; iOS and Mac Catalyst collapse the collection view's layer animations; Windows clears the item container transitions. The AppKit and GTK heads have no native item animations to remove.
- Reduced motion applies, as everywhere else.
